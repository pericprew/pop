1. Information and how it is used

Pop! Buddy is a casual game. The game does not require you to submit your real name or email address to play. Game progress or preferences may be stored on your device, depending on the app version and features.

If third-party advertising is enabled, the advertising provider may process technical data such as device and app information, IP address (which may indicate an approximate region), identifiers permitted by your device settings and applicable law, ad views or interactions, and diagnostic information. This data may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain service reliability.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Pop! Buddy may display ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Where AdMob is enabled, its processing of data is governed by Google's applicable privacy terms and the consent and device settings available to you.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob & privacy.

We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers. We review third-party services used by the app and seek appropriate data protection practices.

3. Children's privacy

We do not ask players to provide personally identifying information directly through gameplay. If this game is offered to children, applicable children's privacy protections and restrictions on advertising and data processing must be followed. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy questions or requests.

4. Data retention and security

Information stored only on your device can generally be removed by clearing app data or deleting the app, subject to your device's backup settings. Third-party advertising providers apply their own retention periods as described in their policies.

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your privacy choices

  • You can review or change iOS privacy and tracking permissions in Settings, when applicable.
  • You can remove locally stored game information by deleting the app, subject to device backups.
  • You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we control, where applicable. We may ask for details needed to identify the relevant data.
  • For data handled independently by Google, use Google's privacy settings and request channels.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if game features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.

1. 我們如何處理資料

Pop! Buddy 是休閒遊戲。玩家無須提供真實姓名或電子郵件即可遊玩。視 App 版本與功能而定，遊戲進度或偏好設定可能儲存在您的裝置上。

若 App 啟用第三方廣告，廣告服務供應商可能處理裝置與 App 技術資訊、IP 位址（可能推知大致地區）、裝置設定與法律允許的識別碼、廣告觀看及互動紀錄，以及診斷資訊。其用途可能包含廣告投放與成效衡量、防止詐欺及維持服務穩定。

2. 廣告與第三方服務

Pop! Buddy 可能顯示由 Google AdMob（Google LLC） 提供的廣告。當 AdMob 功能啟用時，其資料處理依 Google 適用的隱私條款、使用者同意選項及裝置設定進行。

參閱：Google 隱私權政策、Google AdMob 隱私資訊。

我們不會直接將個人資料出售給資料仲介商。我們會審視 App 使用的第三方服務，並尋求適當的資料保護措施。

3. 兒童隱私

遊戲過程不會主動要求玩家提供可直接識別個人身分的資訊。如果本遊戲提供給兒童使用，應依適用法規落實兒童隱私保障及廣告、資料處理限制。家長或監護人可透過下方聯絡方式提出隱私問題或請求。

4. 資料保存與安全

僅儲存在裝置上的資料，一般可透過清除 App 資料或刪除 App 移除，但仍取決於裝置的備份設定。第三方廣告服務供應商依其自身隱私權政策保存資料。

我們會採取合理措施保護資訊，但無法保證任何電子傳輸或儲存方式百分之百安全。

5. 您的隱私權選擇

  • 適用時，可在 iOS 的「設定」中檢視或變更隱私與追蹤權限。
  • 可刪除 App 以移除裝置本機保存的遊戲資訊，但須留意裝置備份。
  • 若我們持有您的個人資料，您可聯絡我們，依適用法律要求查詢、更正或刪除；我們可能需要足以辨識相關資料的必要資訊。
  • 由 Google 獨立處理的資料，可透過 Google 的隱私設定及請求管道管理。

6. 政策變更

如遊戲功能、第三方服務或法規要求有變動，我們可能更新本政策，並於本頁公布新版本及生效日期。
