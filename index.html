Pop! Buddy 隱私權政策

歡迎使用 Pop! Buddy（以下簡稱「本遊戲」）。我們重視你的隱私。本政策說明本遊戲及整合的第三方廣告服務可能如何處理資料。

1. 廣告與第三方服務

本遊戲使用 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）展示廣告。Google 及其廣告合作夥伴可能依其服務設定、使用者同意狀態及適用法律，蒐集或處理下列資料：

  • IP 位址（可能用於推估大致位置）；
  • 裝置識別碼，例如廣告識別碼或應用程式範圍的識別碼；
  • 廣告資料，例如看過的廣告及廣告互動；
  • 產品互動，例如開啟應用程式及影片觀看互動；
  • 當機記錄、效能與診斷資料。

這些資料可能用於提供與衡量廣告、廣告分析、偵測無效流量，以及改善服務。實際處理方式取決於使用的 SDK 版本、廣告設定及使用者的隱私選擇。

了解詳情：Google 隱私權政策、Google AdMob iOS 資料揭露說明。

2. 追蹤與選擇

若本遊戲使用依 Apple 定義屬於跨 App 或網站追蹤的廣告功能，將依適用要求請求 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）許可。你可以拒絕授權，也可以在裝置的「設定」中管理追蹤許可。拒絕追蹤不代表不會看到廣告。

在法律要求的地區，本遊戲可能提供與廣告個人化或資料使用有關的同意選項；實際顯示方式依所採用的廣告同意機制而定。

3. 資料分享與保存

上述廣告相關資料可能由 Google 及其合作夥伴依其政策處理。我們不會將廣告 SDK 收集的資料描述為完全未收集。第三方服務的保存時間、安全措施與使用者權利請參考其政策。

4. 兒童隱私

本遊戲的廣告呈現及資料處理應遵守適用的兒童隱私與年齡相關規定。若你是家長或監護人，對資料處理有疑問，請透過下方電子郵件聯絡我們。

5. 政策更新

本政策可能因遊戲功能、SDK 或法律要求變動而更新，最新版將公布於此頁面。

6. 聯絡方式

如有隱私權疑問，請寄信至 pericprew@gmail.com。

Pop! Buddy Privacy Policy

Welcome to Pop! Buddy (the “Game”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled by the Game and the third-party advertising services integrated into it.

1. Advertising and third-party services

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Depending on service configuration, consent choices, and applicable law, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device identifiers, such as advertising or app-scoped identifiers;
  • Advertising information, including ads viewed and ad interactions;
  • Product interactions, such as app opens and video interactions;
  • Crash logs, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

Such information may be used to serve and measure ads, provide ad analytics, detect invalid traffic, and improve services. Actual practices depend on the SDK version, ad settings, and your privacy choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob iOS data disclosure.

2. Tracking and your choices

If the Game uses advertising practices that constitute tracking across other companies' apps or websites under Apple's rules, it will request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission as required. You may decline tracking permission or manage it in your device Settings. Declining tracking does not necessarily disable ads.

Where required by law, the Game may present choices about ad personalization or data use. The available controls depend on the advertising consent mechanism implemented.

3. Sharing and retention

The advertising-related information described above may be processed by Google and its partners in accordance with their own policies. We do not represent that the integrated advertising SDK collects no data. Please review the third parties' policies for information about retention, safeguards, and privacy rights.

4. Children's privacy

Ad delivery and data handling in the Game are intended to comply with applicable child-privacy and age-related requirements. Parents or guardians with privacy questions may contact us using the address below.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Game features, SDKs, or legal requirements evolve. Updates will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, email pericprew@gmail.com.
